How to play

  1. Each round gives you a category.
  2. Type the longest word that fits it, then press Enter.
  3. Correct answer: your character climbs higher while the lava stays still.
  4. Wrong answer, repeated word, or time's up: you are eliminated immediately.
  5. Correct answers make your character climb. The same rules apply to the bots.
  6. Last player standing wins. Check the live leaderboard for rankings.
