How to play

  1. Three players, three lava towers. You play against two bots, Juan and Maria. You take turns, and each player has their own lava level and score.
  2. Your turn: you get a category. Type a word that fits it and press Enter before the timer runs out. Each letter is worth 1 point, so longer words score more.
  3. The lava always creeps up a little on every turn, even after a correct word. Push it back with long words: 1-4 letters: no push-back. 5-7 letters: lava drops 5%. 8+ letters: lava drops 15% and you get +5 bonus points.
  4. Time's up = lava jumps. Run out of time and your lava jumps, and the jump gets bigger every round. A wrong or repeated word doesn't cost you anything, so keep trying until the timer ends.
  5. Combos: every correct word in a row builds your combo. Each 2 words add +1x to your multiplier, up to 5x. A time-out resets it.
  6. Boss rounds: every 5th round is a boss round. The player whose turn it is must find 2 words in one turn. Both words count for points and push-back.
  7. Disaster events: before some turns a random event hits, like Heat Wave or Lava Surge (extra lava), Cool Breeze (less lava), Volcanic Tremor (less time), Long Words Only or No Spark. Check the banner.
  8. Spark hint: once per game, spend 5 points to reveal the first letter and length of a valid word.
  9. Last Chance mode: when your lava reaches 90%, you get less time but all your points are doubled. Survive!
  10. Winning: if your lava reaches 100% you melt. The game ends when you melt or when both bots melt. Melt both bots to win!
  11. Daily challenge: everyone gets the same categories and events each day, always on Normal. Play on consecutive days to build a streak.
  12. Bots talk back: Juan and Maria react in their speech bubbles when they time out, take the lead, get hot or melt, and they gloat or sympathize when someone else melts.
  13. On a phone: when the keyboard opens, the screen switches to a compact layout so the category, timer and your input stay visible.
  14. Tip: open the gear button for volume, themes, screen shake and the Filipino pack. Some themes unlock through achievements.
