How to play

  1. Each round gives you a category.
  2. Type the longest word that fits it, then press Enter.
  3. 1-4 letters: no push-back. 5-7 letters: lava drops a little. 8+ letters: lava drops a lot.
  4. Wrong word, repeated word, or time's up: the lava keeps rising.
  5. Each round the lava rises faster. If it reaches the top, you're out.
